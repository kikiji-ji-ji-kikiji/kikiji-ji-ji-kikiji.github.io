This project uses Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) backscatter trend analysis, processed via Google Earth Engine, to monitor land subsidence and uplift patterns across Nanjing, Jinhua, and Guizhou, China. The analysis identifies areas of ground deformation relevant to urban planning and infrastructure risk assessment.
