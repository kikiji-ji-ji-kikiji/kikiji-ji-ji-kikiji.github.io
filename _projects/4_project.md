An exploratory project developing methods to detect and characterize informal urban growth using satellite imagery and Python-based geospatial analysis. The work sits at the intersection of remote sensing and urban development research, aiming to flag areas of rapid, unplanned settlement growth that are often underrepresented in official planning data.
