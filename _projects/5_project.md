An in-progress research direction exploring how local climate conditions moderate the regional development effects of High-Speed Rail. The design combines MODIS Land Surface Temperature (LST) as an outcome variable with CORDEX-EA climate normals as a moderating variable, layered onto the same staggered Difference-in-Differences framework used in the core HSR dissertation research.

Currently at the stage of a research design memo and pilot R script, this project extends the causal inference approach from the dissertation into a climate-economic geography context.
