Fear of Living an Average Life

The Weight of living an Average Life — Every morning, I wake up to the same routine: the alarm blare, grabbing an Americano from the Cafe, and to sit in front of the screen. The world inside my phone screen buzzed with life, stories after stories. While scrolling through the screen, a gnawing fear gripped my heart. The fear of living an average life. I watched my friends chasing dreams, while i always felt tethered to mediocrity. Every day felt like a constant reminder of unfulfilled potential. This eventually led to mental exhaustion and a tired body. This daily battle within myself forced me to pause my mundane routine, and an epiphany led me to reevaluate my life’s direction. Journey of self-discovery, vowing to transform my once-average life into an extraordinary adventure began.

A glance in the past- I have possibly lived one of the best lives, People only imagine about having a such life and yet here i am who lived it. Perfect mental peace within myself while growing up, full of confidence in myself, flying without wings and with no fear of failing with the freedom to do whatever I wanted in a South Asian household. Surrounded by books of all kinds, which later became the platform of my deepest understanding of human behaviour. And yet i went through this mental torment at the age of 25. I was calculating every moment, feelings and circumstances which pushed me towards a terrible feeling and phobia of mundaneness. After analysing for a while, i have realised this feeling came from comparison. Comparing my worth and competence with everyone around me. When i was just taking my initial steps and everyone was about to finish the race. This comparison was completely unfair and was unkind to my soul. How did i even learnt to compare myself with others? Nobody taught me this at house, neither i have learnt this in school. I have leant this in the world of competitive people where everyone was running towards … what? To get that chair in the corner of some office but i was never made for it. I always knew I was not made for that.

And Yes! The journey begins. Journey of self reclaimation. To feel and hold that inner peace which i always had. Journey towards struggles which will be completely claimed by me in its entirety. This autumn bought me back to myself. ❤
