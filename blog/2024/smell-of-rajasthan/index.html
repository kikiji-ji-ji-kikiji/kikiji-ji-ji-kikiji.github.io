In the hazy realm between sleep and wakefulness, the familiar symphony of an Indian kitchen gently pulls me from my dreams. It’s a winter weekend morning and the aroma of something special. The heavenly smell of bajre ki roti with ghee wafts through the house.

Time moves differently in those moments, stretching and contracting like a cat in a sunbeam. As noon approaches, the world outside seems to slow down, caught in the drowsy heat of the afternoon. But then, cutting through the sleepy silence, comes the tinkling of a bell. It’s the ice cream seller, and suddenly I used to run despite the oppressive heat. Holding in my hand, a bowl of wheat. A barter that feels both ancient and timeless. The artificial orange flavor in a uniquely nostalgic combination, hiding spot among the mustard seed sacks from the winter harvest. It’s was the secret place.

In this moment, past and present blur together, and I still wonder if in parallel universe, my childhood self is waiting for the sound of the ice cream seller’s bell to ring once more time before it stepping into fast pace life.
