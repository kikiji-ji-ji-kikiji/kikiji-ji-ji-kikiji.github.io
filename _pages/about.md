Hi! I'm Keerti Yadav — a Geography researcher and GIS specialist based in South Korea.

I'm currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Urban Geography at Kongju National University, fully funded by the Government of Korea Scholarship. My research examines the heterogeneous regional effects of High-Speed Rail across South Korea — how transit infrastructure reshapes regional development, connectivity, and socio-spatial transformation at the city level, using a staggered difference-in-differences approach across 2008–2020.

My work is grounded in spatial data: building multi-city models in R and ArcGIS, validating transit datasets across heterogeneous urban regions, and applying QGIS and Google Earth Engine to catch data gaps and boundary errors that automated pipelines miss. I also integrate road network variables into spatial models via cross-join pipelines, experiment with deep learning approaches to strengthen predictive spatial modeling, and have developed urban transport infrastructure sustainability models for Indian cities.

I communicate this research to academic and policy audiences alike, in both Korean and English, and have presented at the KAGIS Fall Conference, the IAGI International Symposium, and national conferences in India.

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Korean, I bring a genuinely cross-cultural, multilingual lens to research — useful when working across government datasets, international collaborators, and multi-country urban comparisons.
